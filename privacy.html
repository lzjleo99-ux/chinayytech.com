2. What we collect, and why

2.1 Enquiries you send us

When you submit the contact form we collect the name, phone number including country code, regional office you select and the message you write. We use this only to answer your enquiry and to follow up about the products or quotation you asked about. Providing it is voluntary, but without it we cannot respond.

2.2 Analytics

If — and only if — you accept analytics cookies, we use Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-EFPZTMKESC) to understand which pages are visited and how people navigate the site. This helps us improve the content. If you decline, or simply do not answer the banner, no analytics script is loaded and no request is made to Google at all.

2.3 Technical data

Our host records standard server logs, including your IP address, user agent and the files requested, which are used to deliver the site and for security. See section 5.

4. Legal bases

  • Your consent — analytics cookies. You may withdraw it at any time.
  • Steps taken at your request prior to entering a contract — responding to your enquiry.
  • Legitimate interests — keeping the site available and secure, and preventing abuse of the contact form.

5. Who else processes your data

  • GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages) — hosts and serves this website, and processes server logs including your IP address.
  • FormSubmit — receives your contact-form submission and forwards it to our inbox. Your form content passes through their service.
  • Tencent (QQ Enterprise Mail) — hosts the info@chinayytech.com mailbox where your enquiry is received and stored.
  • Google Ireland Limited — provides Google Analytics, only after consent. Data may be transferred to Google LLC in the United States.

We do not sell personal data and do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

6. International transfers

We work with regional offices in Serbia, the United Kingdom, Nigeria, the United States and Chile, and with colleagues and factory partners in China, so your enquiry may be handled outside your own country. Your enquiry is also received in a mailbox hosted by Tencent in China. The UK has not issued an adequacy regulation for China, so we rely on the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, together with a transfer risk assessment, to safeguard this transfer.

7. How long we keep it

  • Enquiry emails: 12 months after our last contact with you, then deleted.
  • Analytics data: retained in Google Analytics according to the property's retention setting, which can be set to 2 or 14 months.
  • Your consent choice: until you clear your browser storage.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, to receive it in a portable format, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise any of these, email info@chinayytech.com.

If you are in the UK or EEA and are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you may complain to your supervisory authority — in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

9. Links to other sites

Our quotation button opens aichinatrade.com, and our contact page links to a WeChat Work profile operated through Tencent. Those services have their own privacy practices and this policy does not cover them.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed; material changes to how we use your data will be reflected here before they take effect.
